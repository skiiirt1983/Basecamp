Basecamp

Boot launcher for the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75. Every power-on shows a menu of your apps; tap one to start it.

Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. Other browsers cannot flash over USB.

New install

First time, or start clean. Erases everything on the board, including app settings.

Update only

Replaces just the launcher. Your apps, their order and settings stay.

How

  1. Plug the board into your computer with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Click a button, pick the board's serial port in the popup, and confirm.
  3. Wait until it says finished (a new install takes a few minutes).
  4. Unplug and replug the board. The menu appears.
  5. Join the open Wi-Fi BaseCamp-Setup on your phone (or browse to 192.168.4.1) to add apps.

If the port does not show up, hold BOOT while plugging in, then try again.
